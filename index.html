Loomframe is a 2D animation studio for sketchers, students and storytellers. Draw frame by frame with onion skin, add motion in one click, and export a GIF or video in seconds.

Bounce.loom

Everything you need to bring a drawing to life

Built around how animators actually work, without the clutter.

Pressure-aware brushes

Smooth, responsive ink that follows your stylus pressure, plus eraser, paint bucket and colour picker.

Onion skin

See the previous and next frames as coloured ghosts, so every drawing lines up with the last.

One-click motion

Slide, spin, scale or fade any drawing over time with easing. Loomframe draws the in-betweens for you.

Layers and timeline

Separate backgrounds, characters and effects. Duplicate, reorder and preview frames on a visual timeline.

Export anywhere

Share as an animated GIF or video, or hand off a sprite sheet to your game engine.

Private by design

No account and no cloud. Projects autosave on your device and move between devices as a single file.
